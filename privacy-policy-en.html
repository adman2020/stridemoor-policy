1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Voluntarily

  • Registration Information: When you create a StrideMoor account, we may collect your nickname, avatar, email address, or phone number.
  • Profile Information: You may provide age, gender, height, weight, and other information in your profile to enable more accurate sports data analysis.

1.2 Health & Activity Data

The core functions of the App include run tracking and social features. With your explicit authorization, we may obtain your health and activity data through the following means:

  • Device Sensors: When you use the App to record a run, we may access your device's GPS, accelerometer, and other sensor data to generate route and activity data.

We only collect the above data when you actively use the run tracking feature with your explicit authorization. You can withdraw authorization at any time in your device settings.

1.3 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device model, OS version, device identifiers, etc.
  • Log Information: App usage data, crash logs, performance data, etc., used to improve product experience.
  • Location Information: When you use the run tracking feature, we collect precise GPS location data to generate activity route maps.

2. How We Use Information

The information we collect is used for the following purposes:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the core features of StrideMoor (run tracking, activity analysis, social interaction);
  • Generate activity statistics and personal activity reports;
  • Display your activity records and route maps;
  • Process social interactions with friends (likes, comments, leaderboards, etc.);
  • Send activity-related notifications and reminders;
  • Analyze app usage to optimize user experience;
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements.

3. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We commit not to sell your personal information to any third party. We may share your information under the following circumstances:

  • With Your Explicit Consent: Sharing when we have obtained your separate consent;

  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information as required by laws, court orders, or government agencies.

5. AI Run Analysis & AI Coach

The App provides AI-driven run analysis and an AI Coach feature. The following applies when you use these features:

5.1 What Data We Collect and How

When you actively tap the "AI Run Analysis" (AI Run/Cycle Analysis) button, or turn on the "AI Coach" switch during an activity, the following activity data is transmitted through our encrypted servers (TLS-encrypted) to a third-party AI service provider to generate analysis results or real-time guidance:

  • Activity basics: activity type, start/end time, duration, total distance;
  • Performance data: pace/speed, heart rate, cadence, elevation, calories burned;
  • Route data: GPS track points (used to analyze route and elevation profile);
  • Real-time activity data (AI Coach only): real-time pace, heart rate, distance, etc. during the activity;
  • Your input: text content you enter during AI interactions.

How we collect: Data is collected and transmitted only when you actively use the AI features above. The App never sends activity data in the background. Before first use, the App shows a clear consent dialog stating that your data will be sent to the third-party AI service provider DeepSeek for processing; data is sent only after you tap "Agree".

5.2 How We Use the Data

  • AI Run Analysis: generates personalized activity analysis reports and training suggestions based on your activity data;
  • AI Coach: provides real-time voice guidance and tips during the activity based on real-time activity data;
  • The data above is used solely to generate the result for that session and for no other purpose.

5.3 Third-Party AI Service Provider & Data Protection

We use the following third-party AI model service to process your activity data:

  • DeepSeek — for AI Run Analysis report generation and AI Coach real-time guidance

The third-party AI service provider has signed a data protection agreement with us and commits to providing data protection at a level equivalent to, or higher than, this Privacy Policy. Your data is encrypted with TLS during transmission; the third party processes data only for the specific request, does not store your raw activity data, discards it immediately after processing, does not use it to train AI models, and does not disclose it to other parties.

5.4 Your Control

  • First-use consent: When you first use AI Run Analysis or the AI Coach, you can choose "Agree" or "Cancel". If you cancel, the relevant AI feature will not send your data, and you can still use other non-AI features;
  • Withdraw consent: You may withdraw consent for AI data processing at any time in the app settings. After withdrawal, AI features will stop sending data, without affecting data processed under prior consent.

6. AI Health Analysis

The App provides AI-driven health analysis features. When you use these features, the following applies:

5.1 Data Collection & Transmission

When you actively use the AI health analysis feature with your explicit consent, the following health data is transmitted through our encrypted servers to third-party AI service providers:

  • Basic body metrics (height, weight, age, etc.)

5.2 Third-Party AI Service Providers

We use the following third-party AI model services to process your health data and generate analysis results:

  • DeepSeek — for sports data analysis and comprehensive health assessment
  • OpenAI — for health trend analysis and personalized recommendations

The above third-party service providers have signed data protection agreements with us and commit to providing data protection at a level equivalent to this Privacy Policy. Your data is encrypted using TLS during transmission, and third parties do not store your personal health data — it is discarded immediately after processing.

5.3 Data Usage Limitations

  • Analysis Only: Your health data is used solely to generate the analysis result for that session and will not be used to train AI models or for any other purpose.
  • No Storage: AI service providers do not retain your raw health data after processing is complete.
  • User Control: You may choose to consent or decline when first using the AI analysis feature. If you decline, the AI health analysis feature will not activate, and you can still use other non-AI features.
  • Withdraw Consent: You may withdraw consent for AI data analysis at any time in the app settings. After withdrawal, AI features will stop working without affecting data processed under prior consent.

5.4 Important Notice

AI health analysis results are for reference only and do not constitute medical diagnoses. For any health-related decisions, please consult a qualified medical professional. In case of a medical emergency, please call emergency services immediately.
